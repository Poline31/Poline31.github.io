This section contains completed Cyber Threat Intelligence publications based on publicly available information and open-source research.

The reports/briefs/posts present analytical assessments of cyber incidents, threat actors, attack patterns and related activities affecting Eastern Europe.

Each publication represents an independent research product covering a defined topic, scope and timeframe.

Posts are organized chronologically, allowing the development of observed cyber activity and analytical focus areas to be viewed over time.

The research covers topics including:

- cyber incidents targeting organizations and critical sectors
- threat actor activity and attribution analysis
- attack techniques and operational patterns
- information operations and related narratives
- emerging cybersecurity trends

All publications are based on publicly available sources and reflect the information available at the time of analysis.
