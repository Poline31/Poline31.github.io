# Recent Incidents and Key Observations

My Cyber Threat Intelligence dataset has recently been updated with a few new incidents targeting Eastern Europe. My attention was particularly focused on:
- Poland probes MyDr healthcare software breach potentially affecting 19 million people
- Cyberattack on a Polish CHP plant via Private APN (additional report)
- Latvian officials resign after cyberattack exposes data on 1.2 million people
- Russian military hackers pose as recruiters to target Ukrainian IT workers
- Russian intelligence hacks IP cameras to spy on military logistics across NATO states and Ukraine

Two incidents were attributed to APT44, but in most cases, the threat actor remains unknown.

One important note is that I have the first case reported in Latvia where hackers broke into a government computer system that handles car registrations and driver's licenses.
