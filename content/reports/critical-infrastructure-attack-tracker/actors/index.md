## Overview

The project tracks threat actors associated with cyber campaigns targeting critical infrastructure and organizations across Central and Eastern Europe.

The objective is to analyze actor profiles, operational activity and relationships between threat actors and campaigns.

---

# Actor Database

Each tracked threat actor is documented using structured intelligence fields:

- actor name
- actor type
- affiliation
- active period
- geographic focus
- associated campaigns

---

# Actor Classification

Tracked entities are categorized based on available intelligence:

- state-linked threat actors
- hacktivist groups
- cybercriminal groups
- ransomware-related actors
- other malicious entities

---

# Activity Tracking

Threat actor activity is analyzed through relationships with:

- cyber campaigns
- targeted countries
- targeted industries
- attack types
- associated narratives

---

# Geographic Focus

The project tracks operational regions associated with threat actors, including:

- Poland
- Ukraine
- Lithuania
- Latvia
- Estonia
- Romania

---

# Actor Motivation

Analysis includes identification of possible motivations:

- financial objectives
- espionage
- disruption
- political messaging
- influence operations

---

# Intelligence Value

Threat actor tracking helps identify:

- recurring adversary behavior
- campaign relationships
- changes in operational activity
- regional targeting patterns
