# CTI Dashboard

## Overview

The project includes Power BI dashboards designed to transform collected Cyber Threat Intelligence data into actionable insights.

The dashboards analyze relationships between campaigns, threat actors, targeted regions and information operations.

---

# Campaign Intelligence

Campaign analysis focuses on identifying attack patterns and targeting trends.

Current visualizations include:

## Attack Types per Campaign

Analysis of attack methods associated with tracked campaigns.

Examples:

- DDoS attacks
- ransomware activity
- phishing operations
- disruptive campaigns


## Campaigns per Country

Geographic distribution of tracked cyber campaigns across targeted regions.


## Targeted Countries

Analysis of countries affected by cyber operations.

Current geographic scope:

- Poland
- Ukraine
- Lithuania
- Latvia
- Estonia
- Romania

---

# Threat Actor Intelligence

The project tracks threat actor characteristics and operational activity.

Current visualizations include:

## Actors Activity per Campaign

Mapping relationships between threat actors and associated campaigns.


## Actor Motivation

Analysis of campaign objectives and motivations, including:

- financial gain
- disruption
- espionage
- influence operations


## Actor Type

Classification of tracked actors:

- state-linked groups
- hacktivists
- cybercriminal groups
- other threat entities


## Threat Actor Active Since

Tracking actor activity timelines and operational longevity.

---

# Information Operations Intelligence

The project also analyzes narratives connected with cyber campaigns.

Current visualizations include:

## Narratives per X Account

Tracking accounts and sources involved in spreading identified narratives.


## Narrative Types

Classification of information operation themes and narratives.


## Target Audience

Analysis of intended audiences affected by specific narratives.


## News Type Analysis

Tracking types of information sources and content connected with campaigns.

---

# Intelligence Value

The dashboard helps identify:

- campaign trends
- threat actor behavior
- regional targeting patterns
- influence operation activity
- relationships between cyber activity and information operations
