# Narratives & Information Operations

## Overview

The project tracks narratives and information operations connected with cyber campaigns targeting organizations and critical infrastructure.

The objective is to analyze how cyber activity may be supported by coordinated messaging, influence operations or information campaigns.

---

# Narrative Tracking

Each identified narrative is documented using structured intelligence fields:

- narrative theme
- source account
- date observed
- language
- target audience
- related campaigns
- supporting sources

---

# Narrative Analysis

The project analyzes:

## Narrative Types

Classification of recurring themes and messages observed during information operations.

Examples:

- political messaging
- justification narratives
- influence campaigns
- public perception manipulation


## Source Accounts

Tracking accounts and sources involved in spreading identified narratives.

Analysis includes:

- account activity
- narrative frequency
- campaign relationships


## Target Audience

Identifying audiences targeted by specific narratives:

- local populations
- government institutions
- specific communities
- international audiences


## Campaign Relationships

Narratives are linked with cyber campaigns to identify possible relationships between:

- cyber activity
- threat actors
- influence operations
- information objectives

---

# Intelligence Value

Narrative analysis helps identify:

- coordinated influence patterns
- links between cyber campaigns and messaging activity
- recurring themes across regions
- potential information operation objectives
