Critical Infrastructure Cyber Attack Tracker#

Overview#

This project is a Cyber Threat Intelligence tracker focused on cyber campaigns targeting critical infrastructure across Central and Eastern Europe.

The objective is to collect, structure and analyze publicly available intelligence related to cyber attacks, threat actors and information operations.

Geographic Scope#

Currently tracking activity related to:

  • Poland
  • Ukraine
  • Lithuania
  • Latvia
  • Estonia
  • Romania

Research Focus#

The tracker analyzes:

  • cyber attack campaigns
  • threat actors
  • attack motivation
  • attack techniques
  • targeted industries
  • targeted organizations
  • campaign timelines
  • associated narratives
  • collected artifacts

CTI Dataset Structure#

The project database consists of several interconnected datasets:

Campaigns#

Tracking cyber campaigns including:

  • campaign timeline
  • threat actor attribution
  • attack type
  • motivation
  • targeted sectors
  • affected organizations
  • consequences
  • intelligence sources

Threat Actors#

Maintaining information about:

  • actor identity
  • type
  • affiliation
  • operational activity
  • geographic focus

Campaign Artifacts#

Collecting artifacts associated with campaigns:

  • technical indicators
  • tools
  • infrastructure
  • related evidence

Narratives & Information Operations#

Tracking:

  • emerging narratives
  • influence operations
  • campaign relationships
  • target audiences
  • language patterns

Data Analysis#

Collected intelligence is analyzed using Power BI dashboards to identify:

  • campaign trends
  • attack type distribution
  • geographic targeting
  • sector exposure
  • threat activity patterns

Project Status#

The tracker is actively maintained and continuously expanded with new intelligence.
